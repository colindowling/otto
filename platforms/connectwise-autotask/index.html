Salesforce in Claude vs. ANTS on ConnectWise & Autotask

Salesforce in Claude works because Salesforce and Anthropic built it together, for Salesforce data. ConnectWise, Autotask, and HaloPSA don't have that, and most of the businesses running them also run a separate CRM for the sales side, which no off-the-shelf tool bridges well. ANTS maps into both: the PSA that runs service delivery and the CRM that runs sales, because an agent that only sees one side is guessing about the other.
