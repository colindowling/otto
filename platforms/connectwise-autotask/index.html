The gap

What Salesforce users just got, and what you get instead.

Salesforce in Claude vs. OTTO on ConnectWise & Autotask

Salesforce in Claude works because Salesforce and Anthropic built it together, for Salesforce data. ConnectWise, Autotask, and HaloPSA don't have that, and most of the businesses running them also run a separate CRM for the sales side, which no off-the-shelf tool bridges well. OTTO maps into both: the PSA that runs service delivery and the CRM that runs sales, because an agent that only sees one side is guessing about the other.

Want the longer version? Salesforce just got Claude built in. Here's what that means if you run ConnectWise or Autotask.
