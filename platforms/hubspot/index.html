Salesforce in Claude vs. ANTS on HubSpot

Salesforce in Claude gives Salesforce sellers a plugin with pre-built sales skills, pipeline updates, deal reviews, meeting prep, all reasoning over live CRM data from inside Claude. HubSpot doesn't have that off the shelf. ANTS builds the equivalent directly into your HubSpot instance, and then manages it, tunes it, and keeps it running, which a plugin alone doesn't do.
