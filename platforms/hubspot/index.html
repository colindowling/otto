The gap

What Salesforce users just got, and what you get instead.

Salesforce in Claude vs. OTTO on HubSpot

Salesforce in Claude gives Salesforce sellers a plugin with pre-built sales skills, pipeline updates, deal reviews, meeting prep, all reasoning over live CRM data from inside Claude. HubSpot doesn't have that off the shelf. OTTO builds the equivalent directly into your HubSpot instance, and then manages it, tunes it, and keeps it running, which a plugin alone doesn't do.

Want the longer version? Salesforce just got Claude built in. Here's what that means if you run HubSpot.

What changes

What this actually looks like in HubSpot.

/ 01

Deal data updates itself.

Reps stop manually logging calls and updating stages. Agents update HubSpot deal records directly from calls and email threads.

/ 02

Lead routing that actually reflects your ICP.

Inbound leads get scored and routed inside HubSpot the moment they come in, not batched for someone to triage later.

/ 03

Renewal and expansion signals, native to your properties.

Agents watch HubSpot deal and contact properties for expansion and churn signals and flag them before a human would notice.
