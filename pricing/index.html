Why “Supercolony”

Independent colonies that stop competing and start cooperating.

A supercolony, in nature, is what happens when independent colonies stop competing and start cooperating across a shared territory — some span hundreds of miles. Argentine ants are the famous case. That's the idea at this tier: every worker in every department, working from what the others have already learned.

Pricing questions

The things people actually ask

More in the full FAQ, including security, governance and what happens to your agents if the engagement ends.

Why a 12-month term instead of month-to-month?

Because the work doesn't pay off in 30 days. The first month is deployment. The value compounds in months three through twelve, as workers get tuned against live performance data and the colony adapts to how your business actually changes.

A month-to-month arrangement would let either of us walk away before the work has had time to prove itself, which would push us toward quick wins over the things that actually move revenue. A colony that matters to your revenue, your customers, and your risk exposure deserves a real commitment on both sides.

Is the Blueprint fee refundable, or credited?

Credited, not refunded. The Blueprint is $6,500 one-time, and 50% is credited toward year one if you sign a retainer. The deliverable is yours to keep either way, including if you decide ANTS isn't the right fit and take the roadmap to someone else.

Can I move between tiers?

Yes. Moving up mid-term is straightforward and takes effect the following month. Moving down is reviewed case by case, because the agent count and governance scope in the higher tiers usually can't be unwound cleanly without turning agents off.

What happens if we want out before the 12 months are up?

We look at it case by case rather than enforcing a rigid penalty. If the work isn't landing, we'd rather understand why than hold you to a contract. Whatever gets agreed is confirmed in writing. See the Terms for the formal position.

Are there costs beyond the retainer?

The retainer covers ANTS' work: building, tuning, monitoring, governing and reporting. It does not cover third-party costs you already own or would need to add, such as your CRM seats, enrichment data, sending infrastructure, or model API usage where an agent runs against your own account. Those get identified and estimated during the Blueprint so there are no surprises.
