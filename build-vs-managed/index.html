When building is right

Sometimes you should build it yourself.

Build it yourself when these are already true.

01

Someone owns it as a job

You have a person who can operate agents as ongoing work, not as a side project beside a quota or a close.

02

The scope stays small

One or two agents, a narrow job, and tools that rarely change. A motivated person can keep that up.

03

The motion is still moving

If the process changes every week, write the steps down later. An agent tuned to last month's motion will be wrong by next month.

Questions

Build vs buy AI agents

Should we build AI agents or buy them?

Buy a managed service when you want the work done inside the tools you already use and nobody on the team has agent operations as their actual job. Build it yourself when you have that person, the scope is small, and the tools rarely change.

What does managed mean?

ANTS connects to your existing systems, builds the agents, and stays on them. We watch for breaks, update the steps when your process changes, limit what each agent can touch, and bring in a person when judgment is required. You are not buying a product your team has to learn.

Can our team automate this on our own?

You can automate one step. A managed colony is the set of agents, the steps they follow, a record of where the work stands, and someone who owns it when it breaks. That ownership is the part a one-off automation leaves with you.

Do we have to add new software?

No. The agents run inside the CRM and the other tools you already pay for. There is no new product for the team to learn.

What do we keep if we later run it ourselves?

You keep your data and your workflows. The steps each agent follows are written down, so the work is not trapped in one person's head.
