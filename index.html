Agent sprawl

Welcome to agent sprawl.

Someone on the team built an agent. It worked, so they built a few more. A few months later nobody can say how many are running, what they can access, or whether they still work. That's agent sprawl, and it's quietly becoming the default state of GTM teams that move fast on AI without anyone operating what gets built.

/ 01

Sprawl sets in fast

A rep builds one agent. A marketer builds two more. Within months nobody has a full inventory of what's running or who owns it.

/ 02

Shadow AI multiplies

Agents get spun up in tools nobody approved, holding CRM and API access nobody reviewed.

/ 03

It breaks on an API change

A CRM field gets renamed, a vendor updates their API, and the agent fails silently for weeks before anyone notices.

/ 04

Reps stop trusting it

One bad lead score or one wrong email and the team quietly routes around it for good.

/ 05

It never gets tuned

The logic that worked at launch doesn't hold up once your ICP, product, or team changes. Nobody goes back in.

/ 06

Nobody owns governance or security

No access review, no audit trail, no kill switch. If something goes wrong, nobody can say what the agent actually touched.

How it works

How OTTO works

This isn't a project with an end date. It's a cycle that keeps running, because your GTM motion keeps changing and your agents have to keep up with it.

/ 01

Assess what you already have

Too many AI and agent consultancies shoehorn in new tools without adapting to what's already working. OTTO starts by improving the return on investment from the tools, technology, and people you already have.

/ 02

Prioritize by impact

Which agents will move revenue, profitability, and operational effectiveness the most? That's where we start building, not wherever's easiest or most novel.

/ 03

Iterate, don't stop

New agents and improvements to what we've already built are part of the ongoing work. Every deployment gets reassessed as your business changes, not left to go stale.

/ 04

Manage and maintain, in the background

Security, resource efficiency, and verifiable ROI stay covered while you run the business. We handle it in the background so you can build something that doesn't just survive, it thrives.

Then it loops. What we learn maintaining today's agents shapes what we assess and prioritize next, so the system gets sharper instead of stale.
