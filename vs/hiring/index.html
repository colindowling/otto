Questions

AI agents vs hiring employees

Are AI agents a replacement for hiring employees?

No. Hiring adds a person who can own judgment, relationships, and exceptions. ANTS runs managed agents for repeating tasks inside the tools you already use. The two solve different gaps.

Where does an agent add capacity?

On work that has to happen every time and often does not: the follow-up, the renewal date, the invoice reminder, the handoff. A new hire can do that work after they ramp, during their working hours. An agent does that narrow job continuously, and a person handles what needs a decision.

How should we compare the cost?

A hire is a role: ramp, management, and a gap whenever that person is out or leaves. A managed agent is ongoing operation of specific tasks, with no new software for the team to learn. Compare which tasks are waiting on hours you do not have, and whether a person is the right owner for those tasks.

What stays with our team?

Anything that needs judgment, a relationship, or approval. Agents watch, draft, update records, and flag. They hand the decision to a person.

Do we have to change how the team works?

The tools stay. There is no new system to roll out and no new learning curve. ANTS connects to what you already run and takes on the watching.
