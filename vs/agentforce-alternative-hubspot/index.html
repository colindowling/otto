An Agentforce alternative for HubSpot users

Managed AI agents can run inside HubSpot and the other tools you already pay for. ANTS builds those agents and keeps them running, so the work stays in your stack. No new software. No new learning curve.

Book a Colony Call
Direct answer

If HubSpot is where your customer record lives, the practical alternative is agents that work there, plus the inbox, calendar, and other tools you already use. ANTS connects into that stack. We do not ask you to replace it.

A colony is the set of AI agents we build and run for you. One agent handles one repeating job. Together they watch the work that otherwise waits on someone remembering.

That work is spelled out on Tasks that don't get done.

What you keep

Your tools stay. The coverage is what changes.

Platform lock-in is the expensive part of an agent product that only works inside one vendor's walls. Most companies already chose their tools. The agents should fit that choice.

  • HubSpot stays the system of record. The agents read and update it.
  • The rest of the stack stays too: inbox, calendar, and the tools around the CRM.
  • ANTS writes the steps, limits what each agent can touch, and keeps a record of what ran.
  • A person steps in when the work needs judgment or a customer's approval.
Where this shows up

The same cracks, watched on a schedule.

A follow-up after a call. A CRM field that goes stale while the team is in meetings. A renewal date or a quote that sits until someone notices. Those are coverage problems. An agent can watch one of them all the time, inside the tools the team already opens every day.

  • Follow-up that waits until someone has a free hour
  • CRM updates that slip because the team is in meetings
  • Renewals and quotes that sit until a date has passed
  • Handoffs that depend on a person remembering the next step
Questions

Agentforce alternative for HubSpot users

What is an Agentforce alternative for HubSpot users?

Managed AI agents that run inside HubSpot and the other tools you already pay for. ANTS builds those agents and keeps them running. Your team stays in HubSpot. There is no new software to learn and no separate platform to move the business onto.

Do the agents work inside HubSpot?

Yes. HubSpot stays the customer record. ANTS connects into HubSpot and the other tools you already run, then operates the agents there. Email, calendar, and the rest of the stack stay where they are.

What happens when an agent does something wrong?

Anything that needs judgment or a customer's approval waits for a person. Each agent only has permission to do its own job. You can see what it did, and you can switch it off.

Who keeps the agents running?

ANTS does. We connect your systems, write the steps each agent follows, test them, and keep tuning them when your process changes. Your team does not administer a new product, and you do not hire someone to run it.

Does this replace the people on our team?

No. The agents cover repeating work your team does not have hours to watch. A person still owns the conversation, the relationship, and the decision.
