What this looks like

In practice

Illustrative scenario, not an actual client

Picture a 40-person sales team booking 15+ discovery calls a week. Reps used to walk in relying on whatever they remembered from the CRM. With a pre-call research agent running, each rep gets a one-page brief an hour before every call: recent company news, deal history, and the pain points this specific buyer is most likely to care about. Calls open with relevant context instead of generic discovery questions, and proposals go out shaped around what the prospect actually said mattered, not a standard template.
