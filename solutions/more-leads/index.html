The honest version

In practice

What a lead-gen agent is and isn't

An agent doesn't invent demand that isn't there. What it does is make sure none of the demand you already earned goes unworked. Most teams are not short of signal, they're short of the hours it takes to notice a signal, decide it matters, and act on it the same day.

So the measure isn't volume of outbound sent. It's how much of your addressable signal actually got a response, and how fast. That's the number we tune against every week.
