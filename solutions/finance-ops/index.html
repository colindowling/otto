Not just sales
Does ANTS only work on sales and marketing?

No — that's where we started, and it's still where most colonies begin, but the model isn't sales-specific. If a piece of work has a clear trigger, a clear owner, and a real cost when it's missed, a colony worker can watch it — in finance, in operations, or anywhere else in the business.

Finance
🧾

Invoice & AR follow-up

Watches unpaid invoices, sends reminders before they go delinquent, and flags real collection risk to a human — not every late payer, just the ones that matter.

📅

Contract & renewal deadline tracking

Watches vendor terms, auto-renewal dates, and payment deadlines across every contract on file, and flags what's coming before it hits.

Operations
🎫

SLA & ticket monitoring

Watches support and vendor tickets for anything stalled or aging past deadline, and escalates before the SLA breaks — not after.

✅

Onboarding & compliance tracking

Tracks every step of a new-hire, new-customer, or certification renewal checklist, and flags anything stalled before it becomes a gap.

Not sure which of these fits where your business is leaking? That's what the Blueprint figures out first.
